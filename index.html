Serotype Composition by Year

Lab-confirmed DENV typing · 2007–2025

Which of the four dengue serotypes circulated each year. Switch to 100 % to read dominance at a glance — when a previously rare serotype surges into a population with little immunity to it, the largest outbreaks tend to follow. Click any year to break it down month-by-month below.

Monthly Serotype Dynamics

Proportion of each serotype among typed cases, month by month — exposing within-season replacement, where one serotype hands off to another as the year unfolds. Grey bars show how many cases were typed each month, so sparsely-sampled points are easy to discount.
